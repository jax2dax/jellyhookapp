# Short video plan: TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, LinkedIn (2026-10-28)

Not Reddit, not X (founder). One video, cut once, posted to all four. Every claim is in marketing/claims-ledger.md; the
leads, names and sites shown are the demo's sample data and say so on screen.

## What to film (no camera needed)
Screen recordings of jellyhook.com/demo (the real screens on sample leads), 9:16 vertical, 20 to 35 seconds:
- Record the browser at 1080 x 1920 (browser window narrow and tall, or crop a desktop recording to the demo frame).
- Large captions on every line (most people watch muted). Burned-in text, high contrast, white on dark.
- First second = the hook line on screen. Last second = "jellyhook.com/demo, free, no sign-up".
- Voice: your own, or none (captions + a quiet track from the platform's library).

## Scripts (on-screen text / voiceover)
1. "The lead who read your pricing twice" (the main one)
   - 0-2 s: "Two leads filled in your form. Which one do you call first?" (two lead rows on screen)
   - 2-10 s: open Lina Haddad (sample). "This one came back three times and read pricing twice."
   - 10-20 s: the visit chart, click /pricing. "Here is exactly what she read, and for how long."
   - 20-28 s: the hook / Slack card. "Jellyhook tells your team in Slack the moment a lead like her sends the form."
   - 28-32 s: "Try it on sample leads: jellyhook.com/demo. Free, no sign-up."
2. "Where your form loses people"
   - "Your form gets 100 visits and 10 leads. Where do the other 90 stop?" Show field timing on a lead and an abandoned
     form ("left it at the phone field"). End: "See it on sample leads: jellyhook.com/demo."
3. "Not a session replay"
   - "You don't have time to watch session recordings." Show the visit chart: one picture per visit, pages, time,
     scroll, headings. "One lead, one visit, at a glance." End card as above.
4. Founder story (LinkedIn especially, 45-60 s, face to camera is fine): "I built this because sales teams treat every
   lead like a stranger..." Then the demo clip. Honest: early access, free, looking for the first sites.

## Where and how often
- LinkedIn (best fit for B2B buyers): post from your own profile, video + 3 short lines + the link in the first comment.
  Tue/Wed/Thu 08:00-09:00 PT. Add the Jellyhook company page and post the same there.
- TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts: same video, caption "See what each lead did before they filled your form",
  link in bio (jellyhook.com/demo). 3 posts a week to start (Mon/Wed/Fri 12:00 PT).
- Hashtags (3 to 5): #b2bsales #saleshacks #leadgeneration #hubspot #marketingtips.
- Reply to every comment within the day with a real answer (not a pitch).

## Paid boost (optional, after one video does better than the rest)
- TikTok Spark Ads or LinkedIn "Boost" on that post, $10-20/day for 3-5 days, audience: sales, marketing, founders,
  US/UK/CA/AU. Same stop rule as Google Ads: pay per result, pause any time.

## Measure (weekly, in marketing/metrics.md)
Views, profile/link clicks, visits to /demo from each platform (jellyhook.com's own stats by referrer), sign-ups.
Pass/fail after 3 weeks (hypothesis): at least one sign-up that names a video as the source, or 50+ /demo visits from
social. Below that, stop organic posting and keep only the best video as a paid ad.
