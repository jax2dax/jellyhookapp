# Voiceover scripts (ElevenLabs) for short videos, with the clicks to record (2026-10-28)

Recorded on jellyhook.com/demo (sample data; keep the "Sample data" badge in frame). Every number below is what the demo
shows for these sample leads. 9:16, captions on every line. About 150 words a minute: 75 words is about 30 seconds.
ElevenLabs: a calm, conversational voice; Stability about 40-50, Style about 20-30. Numbers are written as words so
the voice reads them right. "..." gives a short pause.

## 1. "Who do you call first?" (Lina vs Tom)
Shots: Leads list (both rows visible) > click Tom Reyes > his single visit > back > click Lina Haddad > Visits 1, 2, 3 >
click /pricing in visit 3 > end card.
VO:
Two people filled in your form this morning. Same form... same fields. So who do you call first?
This is Tom. One visit, from Instagram, on his phone. Twelve seconds on your pricing page, and then the form.
Now, this is Lina. Three visits in two days. She read your pricing twice, and spent over a minute on your Custom plan.
She also read the case study, all the way down to the results.
That is the call you make first.
Try it with sample leads at jellyhook dot com slash demo. It's free, no sign-up.

## 2. "Know what to say before you dial"
Shots: Lina's page > "Their whole journey" text > Visit 2 chart, click the fintech case study > Visit 3, click /pricing
> scroll to Submitted form (Budget 20k+, "Procurement needs three quotes").

Before I call a lead, I want to know what they already care about.
Lina came in through LinkedIn, two days ago. On her second visit she spent over two minutes on our fintech case study,

Then she came back, went straight to pricing, and stopped on the Custom plan.
And her form says procurement needs three quotes by the end of the month.
So I'm not opening with a generic pitch. I'm opening with the case study she read, and a custom quote.
That's what Jellyhook gives you, for every lead. See it on sample leads at jellyhook dot com slash demo.

## 3. "Stop watching session recordings"
Shots: Lina's page > visit 3 chart full width > click /pricing (the detail panel) > click /contact > switch Visit 1 / 2.
VO:
You don't have time to sit through session recordings. Neither do I.
So instead of a video... Jellyhook draws each visit as one picture. Every page they opened, how long they stayed, how
far they scrolled, which headings they actually reached.
Click a page, and you see where they read and where they stopped.
One lead. One visit. Ten seconds to understand it.
Try it free at jellyhook dot com slash demo.

## 4. "Get told, don't go looking" (mark > hook > Slack)
Shots: Start the guided tour > build "Pricing reader" (time on /pricing, 30 seconds) > save, five leads get the badge >
"Create hook" > pick Slack > the Slack preview > save.
VO:
Here's the part I like most. You tell Jellyhook what a serious lead looks like.
For us, it's someone who spends at least thirty seconds on pricing before they send the form.
Save it... and every lead who did that gets a badge. Five of these twelve did.
Now turn it into a hook, and pick Slack.
From now on, the moment a lead like that sends your form, your team gets a card in Slack, with what they read.
No dashboards to check. Try it on sample leads at jellyhook dot com slash demo.

## 5. "Where your form loses people"
Shots: Lina's page > "Time on each form field" (the longest bar) > the note "They left the form while filling it" > back
to Leads > open Marco Bianchi (also left while filling) > his field timing.
VO:
Your form gets the traffic. But do you know where people slow down... or give up?
Jellyhook times every field. Here, the longest pause was on one field, so that's the question making people think twice.
And this lead left the form halfway, came back later, and finished it.
Most tools only count the submissions. This shows you what happened inside the form.
See it on sample leads at jellyhook dot com slash demo.
