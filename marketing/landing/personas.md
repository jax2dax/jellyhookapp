# Persona cards for the landing-page test

Author: Agent C (buyer research). Date: 2026-10-09. All names are fictional. Pain lines are written in the persona's voice,
informed by the phrasing collected in `agent-a-marketing.md` section 2; they are not real quotes. Each card describes the
person only.

---

## P01: Priya Nair, inbound SDR

- **Role / company:** Inbound SDR, B2B HR software, 120 staff.
- **Current tools:** HubSpot CRM, Outreach, LinkedIn Sales Navigator, Gong.
- **Pain:** "A demo request lands as a name, an email and 'interested'. I have five minutes to reply and I'm guessing which product they even looked at."
- **Arrives via:** LinkedIn message from the founder linking the page.
- **Time budget:** 20 seconds.
- **Sceptical when:** it looks like another tab to check; it promises "intent"; it looks like a manager tool, not hers.
- **Cares most about:** something she can glance at before the first reply.
- **Technical comfort:** Low. Could not paste a script; would forward it to marketing ops.

## P02: Marcus Oyelaran, inside sales rep

- **Role / company:** Inside sales rep, B2B logistics services, 60 staff, quote-request forms.
- **Current tools:** Pipedrive, Aircall, Gmail, a shared Google Sheet of leads.
- **Pain:** "I call every quote request cold. Half are students or job seekers and I only find out two minutes into the call."
- **Arrives via:** Colleague forwards the link in Slack.
- **Time budget:** 15 seconds.
- **Sceptical when:** the page is full of marketing words; it needs a new process; screenshots look fake.
- **Cares most about:** spotting junk before dialling and having an opening line.
- **Technical comfort:** Very low. Has never touched the website.

## P03: Elena Varga, marketing automation owner

- **Role / company:** Marketing automation manager, B2B fintech, 250 staff.
- **Current tools:** Marketo, Salesforce, Drift, GA4.
- **Pain:** "Everyone who fills the contact form gets the same seven-email nurture. Someone who read pricing three times gets the same intro email as someone who read one blog post."
- **Arrives via:** Google search for "see pages visited before form submission".
- **Time budget:** 45 seconds.
- **Sceptical when:** there is no Marketo or Salesforce integration; she sees she would have to read leads one by one; nothing says how it handles Marketo forms.
- **Cares most about:** choosing the right track per lead, ideally automatically.
- **Technical comfort:** High. Edits tracking scripts and form embeds herself, with change approval.

## P04: Tom Lindqvist, in-house CRO manager

- **Role / company:** CRO manager, B2B SaaS (project management), 180 staff, demo and trial forms.
- **Current tools:** Hotjar, GA4, VWO, Google Tag Manager, HubSpot forms.
- **Pain:** "Our demo form converts at 2% and I can't tell which field people stall on. Heatmaps blend everyone together, and I don't have hours to watch replays."
- **Arrives via:** LinkedIn message from the founder linking the page.
- **Time budget:** 60 to 90 seconds; longer if he sees field-level data.
- **Sceptical when:** no real screenshots; vague "insights" language; no mention of page speed, consent or GTM.
- **Cares most about:** field-level hesitation and abandonment tied to people who converted.
- **Technical comfort:** High. Adds scripts via GTM himself.

## P05: Aisha Rahman, CRO specialist at a lead-gen company

- **Role / company:** CRO specialist, lead-generation services company (insurance and solar quote sites), 40 staff, dozens of landing pages.
- **Current tools:** Microsoft Clarity, GA4, Unbounce, VWO, Gravity Forms.
- **Pain:** "We buy traffic per click and sell leads per form. If a multi-step form loses people at step three, I need to know which question did it, today."
- **Arrives via:** Community post in a CRO Slack group.
- **Time budget:** 40 seconds.
- **Sceptical when:** it looks built for small B2B volumes; no word on many sites per account; pricing is vague.
- **Cares most about:** form-step and field drop-off across many pages.
- **Technical comfort:** High. Pastes scripts into page builders daily.

## P06: Daniel Brooks, independent CRO consultant

- **Role / company:** Freelance CRO consultant, solo, five to eight B2B clients a year.
- **Current tools:** GA4, Hotjar, Clarity, Looker Studio, client-owned testing tools.
- **Pain:** "Every new client starts with two weeks of me building a picture of where the funnel leaks. On low-traffic B2B sites the heatmaps are just noise."
- **Arrives via:** Google search for "form field analytics B2B".
- **Time budget:** 90 seconds; reads the docs if the page holds up.
- **Sceptical when:** claims sound bigger than the screenshots; he can't tell what data is collected; no way to add client sites.
- **Cares most about:** fast, credible findings to show clients, and privacy he can defend.
- **Technical comfort:** High, but needs client approval to install.

## P09: Rachel Okafor, demand gen lead

- **Current tools:** HubSpot Marketing Hub, Salesforce, 6sense, GA4, Gong.
- **Pain:** "Sales says my leads are garbage. I hand them a name and a job title and they stop trusting what I send."
- **Arrives via:** LinkedIn post shared by a peer.
- **Time budget:** 40 seconds.
- **Sceptical when:** she can't see how sales would use it; no CRM link; it can't replace or feed lead scoring.

- **Technical comfort:** Medium. Would hand install to web or ops team.

## P10: Lukas Becker, head of growth at a small company

- **Role / company:** Head of growth (also co-owner), B2B accounting software, 18 staff.
- **Current tools:** GA4, HubSpot Starter, Typeform, Notion.
- **Pain:** "We get about 40 demo requests a month. My two salespeople and I argue about which ones are real, and nobody knows what the site actually did for them."
- **Arrives via:** LinkedIn message from the founder linking the page.
- **Time budget:** 45 seconds.
- **Sceptical when:** unclear what happens after the free period; visitor data risks; another tool his team will ignore.
- **Cares most about:** one shared view for a small team, low cost, low effort.
- **Technical comfort:** Medium-high. Has pasted scripts into the site head before.

## P11: Grace Holloway, the sceptic

- **Role / company:** Marketing director, B2B professional training provider, 90 staff.
- **Current tools:** GA4, HubSpot; previously paid for two analytics tools and cancelled both.
- **Pain:** "I've paid for dashboards nobody opened. One tool slowed our site, another was flagged by our DPO. I'm not adding another script on a promise."
- **Arrives via:** Google search, comparing tools; arrives already wary.
- **Time budget:** 20 seconds unless something concrete catches her.
- **Sceptical when:** big claims, "AI-powered", no screenshots, no stated limits, no page-speed or data facts, a free trial that hides later pricing.
- **Cares most about:** plain facts on what it does not do, data handling and site impact.
- **Technical comfort:** Low-medium. Would require developer and DPO sign-off.

## P12: Ben Carter, heavy heatmap and replay user

- **Role / company:** Senior UX and conversion analyst, B2B marketing platform, 400 staff.
- **Current tools:** Hotjar (daily), FullStory, Clarity, GA4, Optimizely.
- **Pain:** "I can watch replays all day. What I can't do quickly is say: of the people who actually submitted, what did they read and where did they pause in the form?"
- **Arrives via:** Product comparison article from a Google search.
- **Time budget:** 60 seconds.
- **Sceptical when:** no clear difference from replay tools; per-lead view sounds like less data; no segmentation or export.
- **Cares most about:** what this shows that his current stack does not.
- **Technical comfort:** High. Manages tags in GTM.

## P13: Nadia Petrova, freelance web builder

- **Role / company:** Owner of a two-person web agency building WordPress and Webflow sites for local B2B service firms.
- **Current tools:** WordPress, Elementor, WPForms, Webflow, GA4, Clarity for some clients.
- **Pain:** "Clients ask why the new site isn't bringing more enquiries. I hand them a GA4 report they don't understand."
- **Arrives via:** A post in a freelancers' Facebook group.
- **Time budget:** 30 seconds.
- **Sceptical when:** no multi-site or client access; client-facing view looks too technical; licensing per site is unclear.
- **Cares most about:** something simple to show clients that makes her work look valuable.
- **Technical comfort:** High. Installs scripts on every site she builds.

## P14: Jake Moreno, wrong-fit visitor

- **Role / company:** Owner, Shopify store selling outdoor gear, 6 staff. No lead forms, only checkout and a newsletter signup.
- **Current tools:** Shopify analytics, Klaviyo, Meta Ads, Lucky Orange.
- **Pain:** "Loads of people add to cart and leave. I want to know who they are so I can retarget them."
- **Arrives via:** Google search for "see who visits my website".
- **Time budget:** 10 to 15 seconds.
- **Sceptical when:** he can't tell if it works with Shopify checkout; no visitor identification; B2B words like "demo request".
- **Cares most about:** cart abandonment and naming anonymous visitors.
- **Technical comfort:** Medium. Installs Shopify apps, avoids editing theme code.
