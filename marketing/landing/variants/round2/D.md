# Agent D: cold outsider review, round 2

Below the hero, the four pages are about 95% the same. Most of the score gap comes from the hero.

## Shared (all four)
- **Believability:** high. "SAMPLE DATA" tags, "not a video", 31 KB, "not published a page-load timing yet", and an honest "does not do yet" list all build trust.
- **Hype:** "One line of code" (step 3 also asks for a form attribute). "If you can add custom code to your site, you can install it."
- **Friction:** "visit" is unexplained jargon until mid-page. "If the interest is clear, narrow the pitch..." is filler advice.
- **Future-proofing:**
  - Facts that will go stale: "Free during early access", "every feature", "does not sync to your CRM or export data", "31 KB", "not published a page-load timing yet", the forms list, the snippet frameworks, "Qualified or Junk".
  - Lines that read as promises: "If you can add custom code... you can install it", "No form yet? Setup gives you one", "type it in your own words", "you get notice first, and nothing switches to paid on its own".
  - "Field timing is recorded for **everyone** who starts your form" is already false for visitors who send GPC/DNT or are held by consent mode.

## Differences that mattered
- **The pain-first headline (R2A)** beats a feature statement (V2/R2B) on desire, and it avoids the false "every".
- **The trust line under the CTA** (all R2 variants) adds a lot over V2, which hides the privacy answer in an accordion.
- **Abandoner value in the hero** (all R2 variants). V2 only speaks to the follow-up person above the fold.
- **The V2 slips** ("two ways" next to three blocks, "people behind it") cost it believability.
- **Not decisive:** "How to read a visit" against "A visit you can read in ten seconds".

**Strongest headline:** "Your form sends you a name. *Here is the rest.*" (R2A)

**Weakest line:** "Read /, /features and /pricing, left for 26 minutes..." It is the first sentence in the sample panel on the first screen, and a cold visitor reads "/" as broken.

## Three exact-wording changes to R2A
1. Sample sentence: change it to **"Read the home page, Features and Pricing, left for 26 minutes, came back and sent the demo form. Longest field: 'What are you hoping to improve?' (41 s)."**
2. "Field timing is recorded for everyone who starts your form, including the people who never send it." → **"Field timing is recorded for every tracked visitor who starts your form, including the ones who never send it."**
3. "## One line of code. Your next lead arrives with its visit." → **"## One script tag. Your next lead arrives with its visit."** (step 3 adds an attribute, so "one line" will get called out).
