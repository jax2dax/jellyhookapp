## Which I would act on
R2C. It names both halves of the value in the headline. It also puts the no-recording and anonymity line where I can defend it to a client's legal contact. That line was the thing V2 made me hunt for. On a low-traffic B2B site, field-level drop-off beats heatmap noise, and I can show it to a client.

I would still be wary of three things. The only form-field screenshot is a made-up lead. "No export" means I can't put findings in a client deck. There is no mention of several client sites under one account. The "not for you" list also rules out A/B testing, which is fine, because the client's own tool does that. I'd sign up on one client's staging site, or on my own site, before asking for approval to install anywhere.

## One change to move me a step further
Add one line near the hero buttons: "Add as many client sites as you need, each with its own key. Findings work on low traffic: you see exactly how many people the answer is based on." That would move me from "would sign up" to "would install today", once I had client sign-off.
