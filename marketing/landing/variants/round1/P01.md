## Which I would act on
V4. The headline describes my morning before I have read anything else. It talks to the SDR first and the form-fixer second. V2 is close, but its headline is about the product, not my problem. V1 and V3 make me work harder or talk to someone else.

## The one change that would move me a step further
Add a line to the sample panel, near the top, saying which pages the person read. For example: "Read: /pricing, /features/onboarding, /demo-request." Then it works as the glance before my first reply. A "Forward this to your marketing ops" link with a ready-made install email would turn "interested" into "would sign up", because I cannot do the install myself.
