## Which I would act on
V4. The hero names my pain, the sample sentence is readable at a glance, and the 31 KB / no cookies / "remove the tag any time" lines mean I can paste the tag into the head myself this afternoon without asking anyone. I would sign up and watch the next few real demo requests before telling my salespeople. The page does not get me to "install today" because I do not yet know what I would be paying after early access, and my two salespeople will ignore it unless it is one click from a lead to the verdict.

## The one change that would move me one step further
Add one line of intent on price after early access, for example "planned to stay under X per month for a small team, with notice first". It would move me from sign up to install today. A close second is a lead list view in the hero panel showing Qualified/Junk marks across all 40 requests, so I can see the shared view straight away.
