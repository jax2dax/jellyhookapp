## Outcomes
- V1: skim and leave
- V2: interested
- V3: would sign up
- V4: interested

## V1
- Hook: "how long every field in your form took them" in the sub-line, and "No screen, mouse or keystroke recording."
- Pushed away: the headline is about leads and sales follow-up, not field drop-off. I searched for field analytics and got a sales-intel pitch. "We have not published a speed measurement yet" and no script size or cookie answer tell me nothing I can give a client.
- Scores: clarity 6, relevance 5, believability 7, ease of next step 6, desire 4, action 3.

## V2
- Hook: the plain sentence in the sample panel ("Spent longest on the field... 41 s"), and the "facts IT/legal will ask for" block: 31 KB, no cookies, local-storage ID, GPC/DNT respected. That is what I paste into a client email.
- Pushed away: the headline is still the lead-visit story. Field timing for non-submitters only shows up in section two.
- Scores: clarity 8, relevance 6, believability 8, ease of next step 7, desire 6, action 6.

## V3
- Hook: "See which field loses your leads", and "including the ones who never send it". That is the exact leak I spend two weeks hunting for. It also has the privacy facts block.
- Pushed away: "so sales can read it before they reply" is not my world. The sample is one made-up lead, not a field drop-off chart for a low-traffic site. I don't know how many starters I need before the field timings mean anything, and my clients' sites get little traffic. Nothing says I can add several client sites under one login.
- Scores: clarity 8, relevance 9, believability 7, ease of next step 7, desire 8, action 7.

## V4
- Hook: "the field where they stopped", though it comes after a long list in the sub-line.
- Pushed away: "Your form sends you a name. Here is the rest." is clever, but it tells me nothing when I'm scanning search results. The drop-off promise is buried. It reads as a lead-enrichment tool.
- Scores: clarity 6, relevance 7, believability 7, ease of next step 7, desire 6, action 5.

## Which I would act on
V3. The headline matches my search, so the first 10 seconds work. The facts block gives me something defensible for a client's legal contact. Free with no card means I can try it on my own site or a friendly client. I would sign up, not install on a client site today, because I need approval first.

V2 has the same trust content but a weaker opening. V1 lacks the privacy facts. V4 has the facts but a vague hook.

## Why I stop short of install
Three things are missing on every variant. There is no mention of multiple sites per account or a way to hand a client a read-only view. The sample is made-up data, so I can't tell how it behaves at 200 visits a month. There is no export, so I can't put findings in a client deck except by screenshot.

## One change to move me up a step
Add a line under the field-drop-off section: "Works on low-traffic sites: here is a real field chart from a site with N form starts", plus "Add as many client sites as you need." A real screenshot with real counts would move me from sign up to install after client approval.
