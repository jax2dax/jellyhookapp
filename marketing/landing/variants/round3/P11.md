# P11 Grace Holloway (sceptic) - Round 3

Outcomes: R2B skim and leave / R3A interested / R3B interested / R3C interested

## R2B
Outcome: skim and leave
Hook: "No screen, mouse or keystroke recording. Visitors stay anonymous until they send a form."
Pushed away: "Every form lead arrives with *the visit behind it*." It reads like a big promise, and "recorded for everyone who starts your form" sounds like I'd be tracking people who never submitted anything. My DPO will pounce on that. Heading "One line of code" is the sort of thing I have heard before.
Scores: clarity 6, relevance 6, believability 5, ease 6, desire 4, action 3

## R3A

Hook: "About 31 KB compressed, one file. We have not published a page-load timing yet." An honest gap is rare.
Pushed away: the hero itself says it tracks "everyone who started your form and never sent it" in the first 20 seconds, before I have seen any reassurance. Alarming before I reach the privacy facts.
Scores: clarity 7, relevance 7, believability 7, ease 7, desire 6, action 5

Pushed away: "We have not published a page-load timing yet." I want a number, and I will not bring this to my developer without one. Also "No cookies" but local storage visitor ID: my DPO will ask whether that still needs consent.

## R3C
Outcome: interested
Hook: "It stays off when the browser sends Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track." Concrete and checkable.
Pushed away: "Your form sends you a name. Here is the rest." A bit clever, "the rest" is vague, and it wastes my seconds. Does not say plainly what the product is until the subtext.
Scores: clarity 7, relevance 7, believability 7, ease 7, desire 6, action 5

## Which I would act on
R3B. The headline says plainly what it does ("See what your form leads did before they sent it") with no cleverness, so within 20 seconds I know what it is, and it carries the CRM-note line that makes it feel like it sits beside HubSpot rather than replacing it. The facts and limits are identical across the three R3 pages, so the headline decides it. I would not install anything. I would forward the "facts IT/legal will ask for" block to my developer and DPO and wait.

## One change to move me a step further
Publish a measured page-load number (for example, Lighthouse before and after on a real site) and put one line with it on the first screen, plus a one-paragraph data-handling note for a DPO: where data is stored, retention, who the processor is. A downloadable DPA or privacy summary link would move me from interested to would sign up. As it is, "have not published" is honest but it is still the thing I was burned by before.
