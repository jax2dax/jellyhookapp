## Which I would act on
R3A. It is the only one where the first screen answers my search: leads plus the field where non-submitters stopped. I would sign up free, no card, and put it on my own site or a friendly client's staging site to see whether the field data is real. I would not install on a client site yet. I need their approval, and the privacy facts (no cookies, local storage ID, GPC/DNT respected, consent mode) are good but I would want them in a client-ready form.

Add one line about agencies and consultants running several client sites under one login, with read-only invites for the client, plus a screenshot of the field drop-off with real-looking counts (for example "212 started, 87 abandoned on Team size") instead of one made-up lead. With that I would go from sign up to install, because it answers "can I add client sites" and shows the finding I would present to a client.
