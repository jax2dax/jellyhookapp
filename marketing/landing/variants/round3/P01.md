## R3C
Outcome: interested (I'd forward it to marketing ops)
Hook: "Your form sends you a name. Here is the rest." Then "Read one lead before you follow up" tells me straight away that this is for the person replying. The CRM line sits under the buttons.
Pushed away: "Read them all before you change a page or a form" again points at managers. And the install is still a script tag I can't do myself. "We have not published a page-load timing yet" will make marketing ops ask questions.

## Which I would act on
R3C, narrowly over R3A. The name-only headline names my problem in seven words, and "read one lead before you follow up" is the sentence I'd screenshot for marketing ops. R3A's subline makes me think about form drop-off, which is not my job. I'd send the link to marketing ops with "can we try this on the demo form?" I wouldn't sign up myself, since I can't paste the tag.

## One change to move me a step
Put a one-line promise for me in the hero, next to the buttons: "Open the lead link from your HubSpot note and see the pages they read in 10 seconds, before you reply." Also add a "Send this to whoever manages your website" button that emails the setup guide. That would take me from forwarding it to marketing ops to actually pushing for it.
