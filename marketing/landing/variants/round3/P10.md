Outcome: would sign up
Hooked: "Your form sends you a name. Here is the rest." plus "Read one lead before you follow up." I can picture it settling the argument with my two salespeople. The CRM line and "Set it up once. Everyone sees the same lead" back it up.

## Which I would act on
R3C. It has the hook with the clearest explanation underneath, and the CRM line in the hero removes my "another silo" fear. R3A has the same hook but a vaguer subhead. R3B is clear but flat. R2B has no CRM reassurance up top.

I'd sign up, put the tag in the head (I've done that before), and wait for the next demo request. I wouldn't install the same day. First I'd check that the tag doesn't slow the site, and the page admits there is no page-load timing yet. I'd also want to be sure it doesn't clash with GA4.

## One change to move me a step further
Put a team line in the hero: "Sales and marketing open the same lead page and mark it Qualified or Junk." That is my actual pain, and it only appears mid-page. Then say what happens when early access ends, for example "a small-team plan, price announced before anything is charged". With both, I'd install today.
