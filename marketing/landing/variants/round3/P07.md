Then give me an export or Salesforce field, even a CSV of lead, source, and pages read. Without that I can't build my monthly report. The GDPR answer also needs to be firmer: "no cookie banner needed in most cases" if that is true, or "here is the exact consent-mode snippet for Contact Form 7 sites." I'd get to "would install today" with those.
