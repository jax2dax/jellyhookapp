# Alternatives worth keeping (backup copy)

These scored well in the simulation and serve specific audiences. They share the winner's body (`variants/R3B.md` + the post-test
fixes in `variants.md`, "Decision"); only the hero differs. Swap the hero, keep everything else.

## Alt 1 (main alternative): R3C, "Here is the rest"

Use if: real data shows sales and small-team managers are most of the visitors, or a 5-second test with real people beats the winner.
Simulation: mean 6.89 (winner 6.95, tie); zero skim-and-leave; the outsider's narrow pick; best for SDR and head of growth.

- Eyebrow: For websites that run on form leads
- H1: Your form sends you a name. *Here is the rest.*
- Sub: See what each person read before they sent your form, and which field the people who never sent it stopped on. Read one lead
  before you follow up. Read them all before you change a page or a form.

## Alt 2 (CRO-specific page, e.g. /for/cro): V3/R2C framing

Use for outreach to CRO specialists and consultants only, never as the homepage (it lost sales and managers every round).
Simulation: best for both CRO judges in rounds 1 and 2 (7.75 / 7.55); polarising for everyone else.

- H1: See which field people stop on, and what the ones who sent your form read first.
  (Rewritten from V3's "See which field *loses your leads*": Agent D flagged the causal claim and the "sent it" antecedent.)
- Sub: Field timing for every tracked visitor who starts your form, including the ones who never send it. Every lead who does send it
  arrives with the pages they read, how far down they got and how long they stayed.
- Must add before using: a real screenshot of field drop-off across visitors, and a line on multiple sites once the 1-site limit is
  decided (both CRO judges asked for these).

## Alt 3: V4 loss frame (short)

- H1: Your form sends you a name. *Here is the rest.*
- Sub (round-1 V4): The pages they read, how far down they got, whether they came back, and how long every form field took them. And for
  everyone who started your form and never sent it: the field where they stopped.
- Note: R3C supersedes it; its sub has an absolute ("everyone") that round 3 fixed.
