# P03 Elena Varga (45-second read)

## 1) What I noticed first
"Every form lead arrives with the visit behind it." That is my search. The sample lead chart looked clever but busy: columns, purple gap, zigzags. I could not parse it in five seconds.

## 2) Where interest rose and dropped
Rose: "See what each person read, how far down they got, and how long every field in your form took them." Rose again: "Before you reply, read what they read... If the interest is clear, narrow the pitch."
Rose: the what-if line "It works with... HubSpot, Marketo... Salesforce Web-to-Lead". Good, Marketo forms are named.
Dropped hard: "Someone follows up on each lead by hand." and "Read one lead before you follow up." That is a human reading dashboards.
Dropped: "You need A/B testing or a CRM sync today" (under Not for you) and "It does not sync to your CRM or export data yet."

## 3) The line that hooked me
"Every form lead arrives with the visit behind it." It matches "see pages visited before form submission".

## 4) My objections
- No Salesforce or Marketo sync. The visit data stays in your tool. My nurture tracks live in Marketo, so it is useless to me.
- Reading leads one by one does not scale. I get hundreds a month. I want a rule like "pricing visited 3x, so send track B", not a chart.
- "Mark the lead Qualified or Junk" is sales-team thinking, not nurture routing.
- No webhook, API or field export mentioned.
- Forms inside iframes may not be readable, and Marketo embeds are often iframes. "Some forms inside an iframe cannot be read" is vague about which ones.
- No speed numbers.

## 5) Outcome
**skim and leave**

Right problem and honest page, but it is built for a person reading one lead at a time. It has no Marketo or Salesforce sync and no automatic routing, so it cannot choose a nurture track.

## 6) What would move me up (to interested)
One line saying visit data can be pushed to Marketo or Salesforce fields (or a webhook/API), plus a dated roadmap for it. Also a clear note on Marketo embedded (iframe) forms.

## 7) My pain and my search words
"Everyone who fills the contact form gets the same nurture, whether they read pricing three times or one blog post."
Searches: "see pages visited before form submission", "send page history to Marketo lead", "behavior-based nurture track form fill", "pass pages viewed to Salesforce lead record".
