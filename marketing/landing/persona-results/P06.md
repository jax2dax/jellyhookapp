Rose: the plain "What Jellyhook does not do" list. Honest, so I trust the rest more.

## 3) The line that hooked me
"See where your form slows people down, and the route converters took." Close second: "Forms people started and never sent."

## 4) My objections, in my own words
- Is it one site per account? I have five to eight clients. Nothing about multiple sites or client access.
- What exactly is collected? "Anonymous until they send a form" then what, an email attached to the visit? I need a plain data list I can show a client's legal person. Only a "Privacy" link.
- Low-traffic B2B: does field analytics mean anything at 40 leads a month? Not addressed.
- No export, no report I can hand over. "Fast, credible findings" needs a shareable output.
- The aggregate view is shown only as a mock example, not a real screenshot.

## 5) Outcome
**interested**
Field timing and abandonment on a small B2B site, with a privacy stance I can defend, is what I want. I would read the docs (privacy, multi-site), but the install friction and no export stop me signing up now.

## 6) What I would need to move up one step (to would sign up)
A clear statement that I can add several client sites, a data-collected table, and a real (non-sample) aggregate field drop-off screenshot. Some indication of how it behaves on low traffic, and a sharing or export route for clients.
