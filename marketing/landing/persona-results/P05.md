# P05 - Aisha Rahman, CRO specialist (lead-gen, insurance and solar)

Time budget 40 seconds. I reached the hero, the "two ways" section, the fit list and the "does not do" list. I did not read the setup FAQs in full.

## 1) What I noticed first (first 5 seconds)
"Every form lead arrives with the visit behind it." Dark, pretty, a lead-page chart with "Time on each form field". My first read: a sales-follow-up tool with a form-timing bonus. Not a drop-off tool.

## 2) Where interest rose and dropped
Rose: "how long every field in your form took them" and the bars "What are you hoping to improve? 41s".
Rose: "Forms people started and never sent." and "Which form field do people give up on most?" - that is my question.
Dropped: "Read one lead before you follow up." Everything is per lead, with a named person (Sam Rivera). We sell leads in bulk.
Dropped: "Built for sites that live on their contact form... Someone follows up on each lead by hand." Our leads are sold on to buyers; nobody on my side follows each one up by hand.
Dropped: "You need A/B testing or a CRM sync today." and "does not sync to your CRM or export data yet." No export means no way to get it into GA4 or our reporting.

## 3) The line that hooked me
"Which form field do people give up on most?" It is only an example panel, but it is the exact question.

## 4) My objections
- Nothing about multi-step forms. Step three is my whole problem, and the field timer says nothing about steps.
- Nothing about many sites or pages under one account. I run dozens of pages. "Create your site" is singular.
- No pricing. "Free during early access" tells me nothing about what it costs at my volume, and "no speed measurement yet" is a worry on paid-click pages.
- Abandonment appears as one sentence. Is it by field, by page, across pages? I cannot tell.
- Looks built for small B2B demo funnels.

## 5) Outcome
skim and leave. It lists my forms (Gravity Forms) but the pitch is per-lead follow-up, with no multi-step, multi-page, export or pricing answer.

## 6) What would move me up (to interested)
A line near the top: "Drop-off by form step and field, across all your pages." Plus a screenshot of an abandonment funnel, a statement on how many sites per account, and a rough price at volume.

## 7) My pain, and my search words
"I buy clicks and sell leads. When a multi-step form loses people at step three, I need to know which question did it, today, without watching recordings of 40 pages."
Search words: form field drop-off, multi-step form abandonment, form analytics Gravity Forms, field-level analytics, form abandonment by step.
