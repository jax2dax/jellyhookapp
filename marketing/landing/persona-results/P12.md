# P12 - Ben Carter (60-second read)

## 1) What I noticed first (first 5 seconds)
"Every form lead arrives with the visit behind it." Dark page, and a chart of a visit on the right. It looked like a journey map for one lead. I could not tell yet how it differs from a Hotjar replay.

## 2) Where my interest rose and where it dropped
Rose: "how long every field in your form took them" and the panel "TIME ON EACH FORM FIELD ... What are you hoping to improve? 41s". Field-level time per submitter is exactly my pain. Also "Forms people started and never sent."
Rose a bit: "It is a chart, not a video."
Dropped: "No screen, mouse or keystroke recording." I read that as less data than my replays. Dropped hard at "It does not sync to your CRM or export data yet." and "You need A/B testing or a CRM sync today." Dropped again at "We have not published a speed measurement yet." Nothing on segmentation. "Read one lead" sounds like a per-person tool, not analysis.

## 3) The line that hooked me
"Time on every form field. Forms people started and never sent." Only that. It is close to my question about submitters and where they paused.

## 4) My objections, in my own words
- Why would I add a tag next to Hotjar, FullStory and Clarity? Nothing says what this shows that they do not. Hotjar has form analytics already.
- Can I filter or segment by source or campaign? Can I export it to GA4 or BigQuery? The page says no export.
- One lead at a time does not scale to a 400-person company with many pages.
- No speed data. "Hook" answers questions, but I cannot judge it from an example.
- Is the form-field timing only for submitters, or for everyone?

## 5) Outcome
**skim and leave.** The field timing is interesting, but I get replays and form analytics today. The page admits no export and no segmentation, so I have no reason to install it.

## 6) What I would need to change my outcome one step up
A side-by-side "what Hotjar/FullStory cannot show" line. Confirmation of submitters-only versus all visitors in field timing. Segmentation by source, and CSV or GA4 export, even on a roadmap with a date. A published script weight.

## 7) My pain in my own words, and what I would search for
"I can watch replays all day, but I cannot quickly say what submitters read and where they hesitated in the form."
Searches: "form field time to complete by converted lead", "hotjar alternative form analytics submitters only", "what pages did a lead read before submitting", "lead journey before form submission".
