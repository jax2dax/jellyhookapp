# P02 Marcus Oyelaran, inside sales rep (15 second read)

## 1) What I noticed first
Dark page, big serif headline: "Every form lead arrives with the visit behind it." Quote requests are forms, so that caught me. Then a busy chart on the right that I did not understand. Looks like a data tool for marketing people.

## 2) Where interest rose and dropped
Rose: "FOR WEBSITES THAT RUN ON FORM LEADS" (that is us). Rose: "Read one lead before you follow up." Rose a bit: "Mark the lead Qualified or Junk." That is my problem.
Dropped: the right-hand chart ("green bands", "zigzag marks", "purple frame labelled as time away"). I could not read it in 5 seconds. Dropped hard at "Paste one script tag" and "Snippets for plain HTML, Next.js and Vite/React". That is not my job. Also "It does not sync to your CRM" - I live in Pipedrive.

## 3) The line that hooked me
"Before you reply, read what they read." Close, but not a real hook, because nothing says "this one is a student, skip it".

## 4) My objections
- I have never touched the website. Someone else has to install it, so I cannot try it myself.
- Nothing tells me how I would spot junk before dialling. "Mark Qualified or Junk" is me doing the work again.
- No opening line for the call is promised.
- No Pipedrive or Aircall link. Another tab to check on top of my sheet.
- The chart looks like a lot to learn. Sample data labels make me wonder whether it is real.

## 5) Outcome
**skim and leave.** I saw my problem in the tag line, but I could not tell in 15 seconds that it would save me a bad call, and it needs a developer. I would maybe forward it to whoever owns the site.

## 6) What would move me up one step (to interested)
A plain sentence at the top: "See which quote requests are real before you call, and what they were looking at, so you know what to say." A simple example like "Read 2 pages, 30 seconds, no pricing page = probably junk". Lines saying my web person does it in five minutes, and that the lead shows up in the tool I already use (Pipedrive/email).

## 7) My pain in my own words
"I call every quote request cold and half are students or job seekers." Words I would search: "qualify quote requests before calling", "filter junk leads", "who is this lead before I call", "what did the lead look at", "lead quality for sales reps".
