# P01 Priya Nair, inbound SDR (20 second read)

## 1) What I noticed first (first 5 seconds)
The headline: "Every form lead arrives with the visit behind it." Then a chart panel that looks busy, with "SAMPLE DATA" on it. The dark page looks like a marketing tool.

## 2) Where my interest rose and where it dropped
- Rose: "Read one lead before you follow up." That is my exact moment.
- Rose: "Before you reply, read what they read." and "Inbound sales, SDRs" is named, so someone thought of me.
- Dropped: "how long every field in your form took them". That is for a web person, not me.
- Dropped hard: "Paste one script tag." I can't do that. It becomes a ticket to marketing ops.
- Dropped hard: "It does not sync to your CRM or export data yet." So the lead lives in a new tab, not in HubSpot.

## 3) The line that hooked me
"Before you reply, read what they read."

## 4) My objections, in my own words
- Another tab to check. I have five minutes per reply and I already have HubSpot, Outreach, Gong and Sales Navigator open.
- I'd have to open a separate lead page for every demo request. No link from HubSpot is mentioned.
- Setup is not mine to do. Someone in marketing has to want this first.
- The chart "readable in ten seconds" is a claim I would have to test. The page looks like it was built for the person who owns the website.
- "Time on each form field" and "Ask Hook" read like CRO and manager tools.

## 5) Outcome
**skim and leave.** The pain matches, but I would hit the CRM sync line and the script tag and decide it's marketing's problem, not mine.

## 6) What I would need to change my outcome one step up
- Show the visit summary inside the HubSpot contact or in the notification email, so I don't open a new tab.
- A one-line plain answer to "which product page did they look at," as text, before any chart.
- A "send this to your marketing ops" button or a short forwardable note, so I don't need to explain it.

## 7) My pain in my own words, and the words I would search for
"A demo request is just a name, an email and 'interested.' I have five minutes and I'm guessing what they looked at."
Search words: "what pages did a lead visit before filling the form HubSpot", "see which page demo request came from", "lead context before first reply", "HubSpot form submission page history".
