# P11: Grace Holloway (sceptic)

## 1) What I noticed first (first 5 seconds)
Dark page, "Every form lead arrives with the visit behind it." Not "AI-powered", which is a relief. Sample lead chart on the right looks like an actual screenshot, so it's not just words. "No screen, mouse or keystroke recording" is right under the buttons.

## 2) Where my interest rose and where it dropped
Rose: "Visitors stay anonymous until they send a form. No screen, mouse or keystroke recording."
Rose: "FREE DURING EARLY ACCESS · EVERY FEATURE · NO CARD".
Dropped: "Will it slow my site down? -> We have not published a speed measurement yet." Honest, but it's the exact thing that burned me before.
Dropped: "The legal call stays yours." Fine, but my DPO will want more than that.
Dropped: "Paste one script tag." That's the thing I said I wouldn't do on a promise.
Dropped: "It does not sync to your CRM or export data yet." I live in HubSpot.

## 3) The line that hooked me
"What Jellyhook does not do." A whole section of plain limits is rare. It's the only reason I scrolled past 20 seconds.

## 4) My objections, in my own words
- No page-speed number. Admitting it is better than lying, but it's still a blank.
- Nothing on where the data is stored, who processes it, or retention. My DPO asks that first.
- No HubSpot sync or export, so it's a dashboard nobody opens unless sales change habits.
- "Free during early access" will end. I'm told I get notice, but no price idea.
- I'm a training provider with a sales team; "SDRs" fits, but who else uses it? No customer names.
- Early product, tiny company, unclear who runs it.

## 5) Outcome
**skim and leave**. I trust the honesty, but I can't take a script to my developer and DPO with no speed data, no data-location facts and no CRM link.

## 6) What I would need to move up one step (to interested)
A published script size and load-time figure (async, kb). A one-page data sheet: where data is stored, retention, cookies or not, processors list, DPA. A HubSpot sync date or an export. A rough post-trial price range.

## 7) My pain in my own words, and what I would search
"I've paid for dashboards nobody opened, and a script slowed my site. I want to know which leads from my demo form actually read the pricing page, without another heavy tracker or a GDPR headache."
Searches: "form lead tracking GDPR no cookies", "see which pages a lead visited before form HubSpot", "lightweight website analytics script size", "alternative to Hotjar without recordings".
