# P07 Sofia Marchetti: persona result

## 1) What I noticed first (first 5 seconds)
"Every form lead arrives with the visit behind it." Dark page, lime button. It sounds close to my search, so I stay.

## 2) Where my interest rose and where it dropped
Rose: "See what each person read, how far down they got" and the sample lead panel with pages in order (/, /features, /pricing, /demo-request). That is exactly "which pages did this lead read".
Rose: "It works with... Contact Form 7... Salesforce Web-to-Lead". My stack, named.
Dropped: "It does not sync to your CRM or export data yet." My leads live in Salesforce and my monthly report is a slide deck, so this hurts.
Dropped: "It does not make you compliant... the legal call is yours." No DPA, no EU hosting, no cookie statement. I would be sent to IT and legal with nothing.
Dropped: "We have not published a speed measurement yet." IT will ask about this.

## 3) The line that hooked me
"Read one lead before you follow up. Read them all before you change a page."

## 4) My objections, in my own words
- I need channel to lead (LinkedIn or Google Ads to quote request). The page only says "where new visitors and converted leads came from" in passing. I can't tell whether it shows campaign or UTM.
- No export and no Salesforce link, so I can't put numbers in my report. Screenshots won't do.
- Another script for IT to approve, and the page says nothing on GDPR, data location or whether it sets cookies.
- It is one lead at a time. I want totals by channel too.
- Free, "early access", unknown company. Will it exist in a year?

## 5) Outcome
**skim and leave**
In 30 seconds I saw a real answer to my pain, but the reasons not to bother (no export, no CRM, legal left to me) came as quickly as the hook. I would not take it to IT on this page alone.

## 6) What I would need to change my outcome one step up
A line right under the hero that says leads are tied to the channel, campaign and UTM they came from. A privacy answer I can forward to IT (cookies or not, data location, DPA). Any CSV export or Salesforce field mapping, even "coming in X". Then I'd click START FREE on a staging site.

## 7) My pain in my own words, and what I would search
"GA4 says LinkedIn sent traffic but not which of those people sent a quote request, or what they read first."
Searches: "which pages do leads read before converting", "link GA4 traffic source to form submission", "Contact Form 7 lead source tracking", "form lead attribution Salesforce WordPress", "GDPR-friendly lead journey tracking".
