## 1) What I noticed first
"Every form lead arrives with the visit behind it." Then the sample lead chart on the right. I clocked "form leads" and "demo requests" straight away. That is my 40 a month.

## 2) Where interest rose and dropped
Rose: "See what each person read, how far down they got, and how long every field in your form took them."
Rose: "Marketing and sales open the same lead page and agree on Qualified or Junk." That is my argument with my two salespeople.
Rose: "Visitors stay anonymous until they send a form. No screen, mouse or keystroke recording."
Dropped: the chart. Four columns, green bands, zigzags, a purple gap. It says "readable in ten seconds" but I did not read it in ten seconds.
Dropped: "It does not sync to your CRM or export data yet." We live in HubSpot. A separate lead page is one more tab.
Dropped: "Free during early access... If pricing ever starts, you get notice first." No number, so I cannot judge cost.
Dropped: "We have not published a speed measurement yet."

## 3) The line that hooked me
"Set it up once. Everyone sees the same lead."

## 4) My objections
- What does it cost after free? No price anywhere I reached.
- It does not talk to HubSpot. My salespeople will not open a second tool, and I will have to nag them.
- Visitor data: where is it stored, who is the processor, is it GDPR-clean? I am in B2B in Europe. "The legal call stays yours" is not comforting.
- It only helps from the next lead on. Forty a month means a slow trial.
- GA4 already gives me paths. I need to see why this is better, not just prettier.

## 5) Outcome
skim and leave. I would register interest but not act now. I got the pain match and the shared view, but a missing price and no HubSpot link put it below the effort bar for the next few days.

## 6) What would move me up (to interested)
A price or price range stated plainly. A HubSpot note or property push, even basic. A named place the data is stored (EU) and a data processing agreement. One screenshot with a real result, such as "x% of junk leads read only one page".

## 7) My pain, in my words
"We get 40 demo requests a month and argue which are real. Nobody knows what the site did for them."
