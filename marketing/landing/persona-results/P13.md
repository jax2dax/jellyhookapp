# P13: Nadia Petrova (web agency owner)

## 1) What I noticed first (first 5 seconds)
Dark page, "Every form lead arrives with the visit behind it." Then a busy chart panel. Looks like a sales/CRM tool, not something I hand to a client.

## 2) Where my interest rose and where it dropped
Rose: "Mark your form with one attribute, or count every form." and the WPForms / Contact Form 7 / Gravity Forms support. I install scripts on every site anyway, so one tag is fine.
Dropped: "A visit you can read in ten seconds." The chart has columns, purple gaps, dots, zigzags and a legend. Not ten seconds for a client. Dropped further at "FOR WEBSITES THAT RUN ON FORM LEADS" plus "INBOUND SALES, SDRS, NURTURE", "HEADS OF GROWTH". That is not my client's world.
Dropped hard: "We have not published a speed measurement yet." I put this on client sites, so that matters.

## 3) The line that hooked me
"It works with ... WPForms ... Contact Form 7, Gravity Forms." Only that, and only half a hook.

## 4) My objections, in my own words
- Nothing about multiple sites or giving a client access. I have maybe 15 sites. Is it per site? Free now, but what later?
- The client view looks too technical. I need one screen that says "your site got 6 enquiries, here is what they read before sending".
- "Invite your team with roles" is for in-house teams, not agencies with client logins.
- No export, no CRM sync, so no report I can send.
- Setup mentions Next.js and plain HTML, not a WordPress plugin or Webflow. Webflow isn't even named.

## 5) Outcome
**skim and leave.** I'm a good fit technically, but in 30 seconds I saw no agency use, no client reporting and no pricing clarity, so I'd close the tab. I might come back if someone in the group vouches for it.

## 6) What I would need to change my outcome one step up
A line near the top like "For agencies: one account, many client sites, share a read-only lead page". Also a plain statement that per-site pricing is fine, Webflow and WordPress setup steps, and a simple client-facing example, e.g. a monthly "enquiries and what they read" summary.

## 7) My pain and what I'd search
"Clients ask why the new site isn't bringing more enquiries and I hand them a GA4 report they don't understand."
Searches: "show client where enquiries come from", "client report website enquiries simple", "GA4 alternative for agencies", "form submission tracking WordPress which page", "white label website analytics client dashboard".
