# P09: Rachel Okafor, head of demand gen (40-second read)

## 1) What I noticed first (first 5 seconds)
"Every form lead arrives with the visit behind it." That is my problem, sales say my leads are just a name and a title. The sample lead panel looked clever, but it was dense.

## 2) Where my interest rose and where it dropped
Rose: "Read one lead before you follow up." Rose again: "Before you reply, read what they read... Mark the lead Qualified or Junk." And "Set it up once. Everyone sees the same lead."
Dropped: "Marketing and sales open the same lead page", because it is their page, not Salesforce. Sales live in Salesforce and Gong. Dropped hard at "It does not sync to your CRM or export data yet" and "You need A/B testing or a CRM sync today" under "Not for you if".
I did not reach the setup FAQ properly. I saw the HubSpot/Salesforce Web-to-Lead line only in passing.

## 3) The line that hooked me
"Set it up once. Everyone sees the same lead." Close second: "Mark the lead Qualified or Junk."

## 4) My objections, in my own words
- My sales team will not open another tool to look at a chart. If it is not in the Salesforce record, it does not exist.
- No CRM sync, no export. So it cannot feed 6sense or lead scoring. Qualified/Junk in here means nothing to my reps.
- It is a chart. I cannot tell how a rep would act on it in 30 seconds.
- Anonymous visitors only, and it only fires on a form. My pipeline has plenty of intent before the form.
- No proof, no customers, no security-grade credibility. I sell to security buyers and I would have to get this past our own review.

## 5) Outcome
skim and leave. The pain matches, but with no CRM link I cannot see how sales would use it, which is exactly what makes me sceptical.

## 6) What I would need to change my outcome one step up
One line on the page showing the visit summary landing on a Salesforce lead or in a HubSpot contact timeline (even a roadmap date). A sales rep quote, or an example of a rep using it. A "send to sales" link or a short text summary that I could paste into the CRM.

## 7) My pain in my own words, and the words I would search for
"Sales says my leads are garbage. I give them a name and a job title and they stop trusting what I send." I want sales to see what the lead did before they call.
Search: "lead context for sales", "show sales what the lead did before they called", "lead quality sales alignment", "HubSpot lead intent before form fill", "why sales don't trust marketing leads".
