# P14 Jake Moreno (Shopify outdoor gear store)

## 1) What I noticed first (first 5 seconds)
Dark page, big headline: "Every form lead arrives with the visit behind it." Small label above: "FOR WEBSITES THAT RUN ON FORM LEADS". I don't have form leads. I have a checkout.

## 2) Where my interest rose and where it dropped
- Rose slightly: "See what each person read, how far down they got" (sounds like seeing visitor behaviour).
- Dropped hard: "FOR WEBSITES THAT RUN ON FORM LEADS" and the chart panel with "/demo-request" and "Book a demo". That is B2B talk, not a store.
- Dropped: "Visitors stay anonymous until they send a form." I Googled "see who visits my website" and this says no.

## 3) The line that hooked me
None.

## 4) My objections, in my own words
- Not for me. I sell tents, not demos.
- I can't tell if it works with Shopify checkout. No mention of Shopify at all.
- It says visitors stay anonymous. I want to know who added to cart and left.
- No Shopify app, just a script tag. I don't edit theme code.
- (Not reached in my 15 seconds: the "Not for you if: You run an online store checkout" and "does not identify anonymous visitors" lines. I'd have found out fast, which would have confirmed it.)

## 5) Outcome
**ignore**

Within 10 to 15 seconds the headline, label and demo-request sample all say B2B form leads, and nothing mentions stores, Shopify, carts or naming visitors.

## 6) What I would need to change my outcome one step up
To skim and leave, I'd need to see "Shopify" or "online store" near the top and some sign it helps with abandoned carts. To go further, I'd need it to say who the visitor is (an email I can send to Klaviyo) and a Shopify app, not code.

## 7) My pain in my own words, and the words I would search for
"Loads of people add to cart and leave and I don't know who they are, so I can't retarget them."
Searches: "see who visits my website", "identify website visitors Shopify", "abandoned cart retargeting", "who added to cart and left", "Klaviyo identify anonymous visitors".
