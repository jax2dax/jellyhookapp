## 1) What I noticed first
Dark page, big headline: "Every form lead arrives with the visit behind it." Then the sample panel with the column chart. It looks like a lead tool, not a page-change tool. I did not see the word Webflow.

## 2) Where interest rose and dropped
Rose: "Read them all before you change a page." That is close to my problem.
Rose: "how far down they got, and how long every field in your form took them." Scroll depth plus field time is exactly what GA4 does not give me.
Rose: "FREE DURING EARLY ACCESS · EVERY FEATURE · NO CARD" (not enterprise-priced).
Dropped: the setup says "Snippets for plain HTML, Next.js and Vite/React." No Webflow. The Webflow-style site builders are not named.
Dropped: "You need A/B testing or a CRM sync today" is under "Not for you." I use HubSpot free CRM. "It does not sync to your CRM or export data yet."
Dropped: "We have not published a speed measurement yet."

## 3) The line that hooked me
"See where your form slows people down, and the route converters took." Close second: the lead-page chart showing scroll past the form.

## 4) My objections
- Does it work with Webflow native forms? Not stated. The "What if" list names HubSpot, WordPress, Marketo, but not Webflow. I would have to guess.
- Is it before/after? My question is "did the rebuild hurt leads?" The page is about single leads. Comparing the old services page with the new one is not shown.
- No CRM sync means my leads would live in two places.
- Early access means I do not know what it costs later.
- I only get the visit once a form is sent. I wanted to know how many people scrolled to the form and did not submit. "Forms people started and never sent" is a half answer.

## 5) Outcome
skim and leave. The idea is relevant, but with no Webflow mention and no clear before/after view of a page change, I would not spend my next minute here.

## 6) What would move me one step up (to interested)
A line that says it works with Webflow native forms and the custom code embed, with a paste-in step. Plus a plain answer on whether I can compare lead behaviour before and after I changed a page, and whether people who scrolled to the form but did not submit are counted.
