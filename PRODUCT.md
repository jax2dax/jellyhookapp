# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Primary: people at small B2B companies whose website exists to get demo, contact or quote forms. A founder or a sales
person follows up on each lead and wants to know which leads to call first.

## Product Purpose
Jellyhook is one script plus a dashboard. You can see what each lead did on your site before they filled in the form
(pages read, time, scroll, which headings they reached, time per form field), and where your form loses the people who
start it. Marks label leads by what they did; a hook tells the team (in the app, Slack or a webhook) when a new lead has
the marks it asks for. Success right now: about 10 real sites installed and used. Not revenue.

## Positioning
Lead-level visit context tied to the form submission itself, delivered to the sales person at the moment the form is sent,
instead of anonymous site analytics or a video session replay.

## Operating Context
A sales person reads a Slack ping or the leads table, opens one lead, and decides whether to call now. The founder or
marketer installs the script and sets marks and hooks.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Allowed claims live in `marketing/claims-ledger.md`; banned ones in `marketing/banned-claims.md`.
- Not marketed: lead confidence, `/platform/intent`, Engagement Score, Acquisition (not in the ledger yet).
- The visit chart is a chart, not a video. Never call it session replay.
- Free during early access.

## Brand Commitments
- The name Jellyhook, and the words "hook" and "marks".
- Copy: no em dashes, no hype words (unlock, seamless, powerful, revolutionary), short sentences, plain words.
- The home page keeps the interactive demo and guided tour, the hook input box (`components/showcase/HeroHookBox.tsx`,
  as its own presentation on the right of the hero) and the Slack showcase (`components/showcase/SlackDesktopShowcase.tsx`,
  further down). The founder will supply new page copy.
- The current black + lime look and the current fonts are rejected by the founder (2026-10-28).

## Evidence on Hand
No customers, testimonials, logos, results or usage numbers exist. None may be invented.

## Product Principles
1. Show the lead, not the dashboard: the proof is one real-looking lead's visit.
2. Honest over impressive: every claim is in the ledger.
3. The sales person's moment (a form just came in) is the scene everything serves.
